# Artwork 002 — Kijani

**Status:** ready · locked 2026-06-24 · **Style:** [Sakina](./STYLE.md) · *(the first locked Sakina wallpaper)*

*Kijani* (Swahili: green). A soft painting — the memory of a dream of a weathered green-and-white mosque
wall in a Nakuru maize town, a pointed mihrab arch ghosted into faded plaster. Soft, calm, it recedes.

*(002 became Sakina's **painterly** direction — a faded-fresco memory of a mosque wall — rather than the
flat girih of 001. It carries Sakina through its calm register, faded palette, and the mihrab-arch energy;
it does not use the flat `--sref`.)*

## Subject

A flat, head-on **painting** of a faded green-and-white mosque wall, like a faded fresco — a single
pointed **mihrab arch** ghosted into weathered plaster. Hazy and indistinct, edges dissolving into mist,
peeling paint and dust only suggested. Mostly quiet wall; the arch is a soft presence, not a hard outline.
No room, no perspective, no figures — the wall *remembered*, not the building.

## Palette

Faded **sage-green + chalky white + soft dust** (the weathered-Nakuru mosque-wall palette,
[research.md](../../research.md)). Very low saturation, low contrast — calm, with a faint warm note so it
breathes.

## Medium

A **soft dreamlike painting — a faded fresco**: gentle painterly brushwork, gouache and oil washes, hazy,
edges dissolving into mist. (A watercolour-on-paper route was tried along the way; the keeper is the
gouache/oil fresco.)
