Sakina

سكينة · the calm that settles

The energy, not the depiction — geometry that asks nothing. The Alhambra's restraint meeting the Swahili coast's warmth, in calm desaturated colour.

Palette

Calm colour, earned

Composition

No focal point, dissolving into void

Low-density girih — the pattern is the subject, large-scale and open, fading at the edges so the eye never locks and the screen stays roomy.

A sketch of the direction — not the artwork. The wallpapers are made by hand in Midjourney.

Texture

Matte, weathered, a quiet ground

Texture is an all-over ground, never a subject — the matte side of each tradition.

  • Matte carved plaster · Alhambra yesería — chalky, low relief, soft self-shadow.
  • Limewashed coral wall · Swahili coast — weathered, sun-bleached, gently porous.
  • Soft hand-pressed plaster · Seamless, faint wabi-sabi grain, near-matte.
  • Fine woven palm tooth · Optional mkeka grain — a subtle all-over tooth, like paper.
