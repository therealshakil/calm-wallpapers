# Artwork 001 — Star lattice

**Status:** draft · **Date:** 2026-06-24 · **Style:** [Sakina](./STYLE.md)

One quiet artwork: a low-density eight-point star lattice (*girih*), Alhambra in lineage, dissolving into
a limewashed plaster field. The first of this style.

## Subject (Layer C)

A large-scale interlacing **eight-point star geometry** rendered in fine low relief on a matte plaster
wall — open and low-density, the lattice fading softly into empty ground rather than filling the frame.
No focal point: even rhythm, nothing begins or ends inside the image. The calm is in the breathing room.

## Not this

Not a centred medallion (a focal point pulls the eye — keep it even and dissolving). Not a dense
edge-to-edge rug (no breathing room, no space for icons). No glazed-tile sheen, no gold, no muqarnas
detail, no literal mosque.
