# Artwork 001 — Crescent moon

**Status:** draft · **Date:** 2026-06-24 · **Style:** [ink on paper, Peanuts](./STYLE.md)

One quiet artwork: a small crescent moon, low in a wide empty sky. The first of the set.

## Subject (Layer C)

A thin crescent moon, small, sitting **low and to one side**, with the rest of the frame quiet sky. A
single bird or one faint star is allowed if the frame still reads as mostly empty. Slow, still, wistful —
the moon someone left out for you. No face, no motion.

## Not this

Not a full moon (too much mass, too central a pull). Not a night *scene* with detail — just the moon and
the quiet. No stars-field, no clouds-busy sky.
