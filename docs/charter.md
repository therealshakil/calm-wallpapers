# calm-wallpapers — Charter

> A wallpaper is the one picture you see most days and look at least.
> This set is made to be glanced past — to sit in the corner of your eye and ask nothing.

calm-wallpapers inherits the [Calm Constitution](https://github.com/shakilclark/calm/blob/main/CONSTITUTION.md),
the [identity](https://github.com/shakilclark/calm/blob/main/docs/identity.md), and the
[voice](https://github.com/shakilclark/calm/blob/main/docs/calm-language.md) — read those first. This
charter covers only what is specific here: the one job, the form, and how a wallpaper earns its place.

---

## 1. The one job

**A small set of calm desktop wallpapers, made by hand, that quiet a screen instead of decorating it.**

Not a gallery to browse, not a generator to run. A finite handful you set once and forget — the
calm equivalent of a blank wall you happen to like.

## 2. The form: a finite set, made by hand

- **Finite (Article I).** A handful, not a feed. The set has an end you can reach. No "more like this",
  no infinite variations, no daily drop.
- **Made by hand (Article IX, and the point of it).** A person uses Midjourney as a craft tool — see
  [`the-midjourney-exception.md`](./the-midjourney-exception.md). Nothing automated reaches for the
  tool; no fragile dependency ships. The artifacts are plain image files.
- **Glanceable, not operable (Article I).** A wallpaper is the most peripheral surface there is. The
  job is to recede. Anything that pulls the eye back — high contrast, motion, a busy field — fails.

## 3. The look, in one line

The suite identity in still form: **monochrome ink on warm paper** — black ink on warm off-white
(light), bone-white ink on soft off-black (dark), never pure `#000`/`#fff`. Gentle hand-drawn
pen-and-ink linework, few confident marks, generous empty space. No colour, no gradient, no text.
Peanuts-gentle, never loud. The craft of getting there lives in [`recipe.md`](./recipe.md).

## 4. How a wallpaper earns its place

Subtraction is the default: the set stays small, and a piece is removed as readily as added. The bar
and the four-line check live in [`curation.md`](./curation.md).

## 5. Non-goals

A browsable archive. Colour. Photography. Logos, quotes, or text on the image. A download site or any
public surface. A generator, plugin, or anything that runs on its own. Anything that makes you open
this repo more than the once it takes to pick a picture.

---

## Constitution Check

1. **Serves:** I (a finite set, glanceable not operable — a wallpaper is the periphery itself),
   VI (calm over alarm: monochrome, almost no contrast, no motion), VIII (subtract by default; the
   set stays small), IX (made by hand; the artifacts are plain files, no fragile dependency).
2. **Violates:** none — confirmed vs Articles + banned patterns. The "not Midjourney" line in
   identity.md is about *automated* generation in a shipped app; hand-use is a different thing
   ([`the-midjourney-exception.md`](./the-midjourney-exception.md)).
3. **Subtracts:** replaces a noisy or default desktop with quiet, and resists becoming a gallery — the
   whole charter is a guard against this growing into a feed.
4. **Calm:** the empty state is the goal, not an edge case; no motion to honour `prefers-reduced-motion`
   against; a quiet desktop is the happy path and the sad path both.
