A small, hand-made set of monochrome desktop wallpapers — black ink on warm paper, made to recede.
The kind of picture you set once and stop noticing, which is the point.

This is the first project built the calm way *outside* the [calm suite](https://github.com/shakilclark/calm).
It adds calm to itself by inheriting direction rather than restating it — see
[`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md) for how that works, and the suite's
[Constitution](https://github.com/shakilclark/calm/blob/main/CONSTITUTION.md) for the law it answers to.

## The look

Monochrome, always. Black ink on warm off-white, or bone-white ink on soft off-black — never pure
black or white. Few confident hand-drawn marks, a small subject low in the frame, and a lot of quiet
space. No colour, no gradient, no text. Gentle and a little wistful; nothing that grabs you.

## Using one

Browse `wallpapers/`, pick one at your screen's ratio (`light/` or `dark/`), and set it as your
desktop background. That's the whole interaction — there's nothing to install and nothing that runs.

## Making one

The wallpapers are made by hand in Midjourney. Each artwork is a spec, grouped under a calm style — the
first style is [ink on paper, Peanuts](./docs/specs/ink-peanuts/STYLE.md). The shared method:

- [`docs/recipe.md`](./docs/recipe.md) — the look, the parameters and why, and how to keep a set
  consistent. The calm making-ritual is here too: finite session, no reroll treadmill.
- [`docs/prompt-ladder.md`](./docs/prompt-ladder.md) — the 4-layer process (calm base → style → subject →
  device) and the per-device export targets.
- [`docs/specs/`](./docs/specs/) — the styles and their artwork specs.
- [`docs/the-midjourney-exception.md`](./docs/the-midjourney-exception.md) — why hand-use of Midjourney
  is calm, even though the suite's identity says "not Midjourney" for *automated* generation.

## What this is, and isn't

It's a finite set you glance past. It is **not** a gallery to browse, a generator to run, a download
site, or a feed. The [charter](./docs/charter.md) holds that line; [curation](./docs/curation.md) keeps
the set small.
